# ESPN.com Design Description

The homepage of ESPN.com is dynamic, visually bold, and structured to deliver a high-energy sports news experience. The layout features a top navigation bar with prominent links and a live score ticker that immediately engage the user. Below the navigation, a large hero section displays big, eye-catching images and headlines emphasizing featured stories or live events. The content is divided into clear zones, including breaking news, featured videos, scores, and various sports categories, structured in grids and horizontal carousels for swift browsing.

The visual style uses a palette dominated by a vibrant, energetic red (#FF0000) as the primary color, creating bold accents and drawing attention to key elements such as headlines, buttons, and icons. The secondary color is a deep charcoal black (#0B0B0B), used extensively for backgrounds to provide strong contrast and a sleek modern feel. White (#FFFFFF) is the accent color, applied to text, interactive elements, and overlays to ensure readability and crisp clarity. Typography is strong, modern, and sans-serif, with large headings and clear hierarchy. The overall energy is fast-paced and intense but balanced by clean spacing, making it easy for users to scan and navigate quickly.

## Color Palette

- **Primary Color:** `#FF0000` (ESPN Red)
- **Secondary Color:** `#0B0B0B` (Deep Black Charcoal)
- **Accent Color:** `#FFFFFF` (White)
